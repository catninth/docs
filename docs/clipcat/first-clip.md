---
title: Your first clip
description: Turn on ClipCat's replay buffer, save recent footage, and find the resulting video.
---

Set up capture once, then save a moment with **Alt+F10**.

## Choose where and what to capture

1. Open **Settings** and use **Browse** next to **Save folder** to choose where videos go.
2. Set **Replay length**. The default is **2 minutes 30 seconds**.
3. Leave **H.264**, **1080p**, and **60 FPS** for an initial test, or choose lighter settings for your system.
4. Review **Capture desktop**. On Windows it captures the primary monitor when no full-screen game is running. On Linux, screen capture is the available capture path.
5. Leave **Microphone** off, or select **Push-to-talk** / **Always on** if you want your voice.
6. Select **Save** when you have changed settings.

:::caution Desktop capture includes what is visible
Before enabling replay or recording, close private windows and check your capture choice. Desktop capture is enabled in the default settings. The microphone is disabled by default.
:::

## Enable replay

Turn on the replay switch in the sidebar. A new installation starts with it off. Wait for **Replay buffer running**, then give the buffer time to fill. You can only save footage captured since it started, up to the configured length.

## Save the moment

Press **Alt+F10** or select **Save now**. Wait for the saved notification. Disk buffering may take a few seconds to assemble the video; avoid repeatedly triggering Save while one is in progress.

## Find the result

Open **Gallery** to play the clip, or press **Alt+F11** to open the latest clip's folder. Clips are grouped into subfolders by detected game, with unrecognized captures grouped as **Other** in the gallery.
